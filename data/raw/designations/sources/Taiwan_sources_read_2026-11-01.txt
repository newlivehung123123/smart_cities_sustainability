Sources for Taiwan, read 1 Nov 2026. No designation list is built for Taiwan: its national smart city programme names no
place and selects no place. scripts/designations/09_taiwan.py reads the saved text (taiwan_saved/*.txt, made from the PDF
or html saved the same day), checks the passages and counts quoted below and writes no file.
Taiwan is not an ASEAN member, so it has no ASEAN Smart Cities Network member in ascn.csv.

Coding notes:
- The programme funds firms to run projects with local governments. It names no place as a smart city, and every one of
  Taiwan's 22 counties and cities sits on the Executive Yuan platform that runs it (sources 2 and 3). Taiwan is therefore
  coded as a nationwide programme from 2018, not as a place designation, and no rows are written.
- Under economy-by-year fixed effects a programme that reaches all of an economy's units in the same year is absorbed by
  the economy-year effects. Taiwan has no designated place, so it adds no identifying variation to the main estimate:
  its districts are never treated, and their untreated observations inform only Taiwan's own fixed effects.
- No county-level staggered coding is built from the annex. 28 of the 55 local titles of 2018 name no county, so the first
  year a county is named measures title wording, not when the county joined; Taipei, first named in 2020, already sat on
  the platform with all 22 local governments in 2019 (source 2).
- Taiwan's districts stay in the donor pool for the synthetic control and synthetic difference-in-differences estimates for
  Singapore and Hong Kong, because no place in Taiwan is designated. Every county took part in a national smart city
  programme from 2018, so a sensitivity check drops Taiwan's districts from the donor pool.
