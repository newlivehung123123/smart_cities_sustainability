Sources for Vietnam, read 1 Nov 2026. No designation list is built for Vietnam: no national government list naming smart
city places was found. Vietnam's treated places are the three ASEAN Smart Cities Network members in ascn.csv (Hanoi, Ho
Chi Minh City, Da Nang, 2018). Saved copies are in vietnam_saved/ (PDF or html with a txt copy).

[1] Decision 950/QĐ-TTg, approving the scheme for sustainable smart urban development 2018 to 2025 with orientation to 2030.
Official text as published by Quang Trung Software City (a state enterprise of Ho Chi Minh City),
https://www.qtsc.com.vn/uploads/files/2018/08/07/Quyet-dinh-950-cua-Thu-tuong-de-an-phat-trien-DTTM-ben-vung-VN-2018-2025-va-dinh-huong-den-2030.pdf
(saved as QD950_2018_qtsc.pdf). "Số: 950/QĐ-TTg Hà Nội, ngày 01 tháng 08 năm 2018".
Targets to 2025: "Thực hiện giai đoạn 1 thí điểm phát triển đô thị thông minh"; "Hỗ trợ ít nhất 6 đô thị/6 vùng kinh tế
phê duyệt Đề án tổng thể về phát triển đô thị thông minh"; "tại tối thiểu 3 đô thị từ loại II trở lên". No place is named
for the pilot phase.
Orientation to 2030: "Hình thành các chuỗi đô thị thông minh khu vực phía Bắc, miền Trung, phía Nam và vùng đồng bằng sông
Cửu Long, lấy thành phố Hà Nội, thành phố Hồ Chí Minh, thành phố Đà Nẵng, thành phố Cần Thơ là hạt nhân".
Tasks: the Ministry of Construction is to "lựa chọn, trình cấp có thẩm quyền phê duyệt khu vực thực hiện thí điểm và
chương trình, kế hoạch thực hiện thí điểm", that is, pilot areas were to be selected and approved later.

Coding notes:
- Vietnam has a national framework but no national selection of places before 2025. Smart city schemes were adopted by
  each province on its own (34 approved by February 2023, 37 by August 2025), which is self-selection, not designation,
  and the dates and areas of these provincial schemes are not collected. The four "hạt nhân" cities of 2030 are an
  orientation, not a designation with a programme, and are not coded.
- Vietnam stays in the panel: its ASEAN Smart Cities Network members are treated from 2018 and its other urban districts
  are not-yet or never treated under national designation. Because most provinces adopted their own schemes from about
  2019, Vietnam's untreated districts are weaker controls; a sensitivity check drops Vietnam's untreated districts from
  the control group.
- The 19 provinces piloting smart city services under the information ministry's guidance are not named in any source
  found and are not coded.
