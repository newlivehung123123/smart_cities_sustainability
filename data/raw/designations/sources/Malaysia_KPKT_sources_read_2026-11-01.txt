Coding notes:
- One row per place per programme round (29 rows). The treatment table later takes each place's earliest year.
- MSCF pilots are coded implementation support, as Indonesia's programme is: the framework assigns each pilot city
  project briefs with lead agencies. No national transfer is stated. Date is the launch, 23 September 2019, so the first
  treated year is 2020. Four of the five (Kuala Lumpur, Johor Bahru, Kota Kinabalu, Kuching) joined the ASEAN Smart
  Cities Network in April 2018 and are treated from 2018 by ascn.csv; Kulim is the only place the framework adds. The
  pilot area of each city is its listed councils (area_as_printed), linked to districts when units are built; Johor Bahru
  covers MBJB, MBIP and MPPG (close to Johor Bahru district) and Kulim covers the Kulim council and Kulim Hi-Tech Park
  (close to Kulim district).
- The rating is coded certification: a voluntary rating of councils against MS ISO 37122:2019, with a RM200,000
  allocation to rated councils in 2023 and project allocations to rated councils in 2024.
- 2023 round, dated 9 November 2023, first treated year 2024. Only 4 of 25 to 29 rated councils are named: Kajang and
  Ipoh from their own pages; Johor Bahru and Putrajaya because they were rated Tahap 2 in 2024, which only councils rated
  Tahap 1 in 2023 could apply for. All four are flagged "roster: partial". KPKT counts 29 recipients and the Kajang page
  25; the gap may be awards other than Tahap 1, which the saved text does not settle.
- Main specification: Malaysian unit-years in 2024 are dropped, because about 25 to 29 councils entered the rating on
  9 November 2023 and most are unnamed, so in 2024 rated and unrated districts cannot be told apart. A sensitivity check
  keeps Malaysia 2024 with the 4 named councils treated.
- 2024 round, dated 28 October 2024, first treated year 2025, after the panel (outcomes end in 2024). The 20 rows are
  kept for documentation and so that no 2024-rated council is used as a never-treated control in a later extension.
  Johor Bahru and Putrajaya appear in both rounds.
- place_en translates the council type (Majlis Bandaraya = City Council, Majlis Perbandaran = Municipal Council, Majlis
  Daerah = District Council, Perbadanan = Corporation); "Langkawi Bandaraya Pelancongan" is Langkawi's tourism-city title.
  Kuching Selatan is Kuching South. Councils are linked to districts against the boundary file when units are built.
