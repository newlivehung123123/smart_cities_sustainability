Passages are copied from the page text after NFKC normalisation. The script parses every roster from the saved text and
stops if any list differs from its province table, so the lists below are the ones coded.

Access: komdigi.go.id (formerly kominfo.go.id) refused direct downloads, so its articles were read in a browser from the
JSON feed the site's own pages load, and the passages used are in indonesia_saved/komdigi_api_extract_2026-11-01.txt.
aptika.kominfo.go.id no longer resolves; its two pages are Wayback Machine copies. Other pages were saved as html with a
txt copy (totxt_helper.py). Not saved: banjarkota.go.id (behind a Cloudflare check), the suarapemerintah.id pages (the
Wayback copies were interstitial pages) and taburapos.co; these are cited from search results only and code nothing on
their own. tribun_2019.html is a 403 page and is not used. Licences of the saved news pages (Antara forbids crawling for
AI training) are to be checked before any raw file is pushed.

Bappenas and the Presidential Staff Office (Siaran Pers 223/2017). Places sign an MoU and are guided to a ten-year smart
city master plan and quick wins; no direct transfer was found. Coded as implementation support.

Coding notes:
- One row per place per round (262 rows, 251 places). The treatment table later takes each place's earliest year.
- Date is the MoU signing. First treated year is that year if the month is June or earlier, otherwise the next year.
  2019 has no MoU date found; 15 May 2019 is the latest date by which the round was under way, so it codes 2019.
- 2017 and 2018 have both an MoU list and an award list, and they differ. Places on both are coded to the MoU date.
  Places on the MoU list only (2017 Singkawang, Purwakarta; 2018 Banyumas, Depok, Kudus) keep the MoU date and are
  flagged "roster: mou only"; a sensitivity check drops them. Places on the award list only (2017 Lombok Timur; 2018
  Padang Panjang, Kota Pekalongan, Kabupaten Cirebon) take the award date, so their first treated year is the next year.
- Other rounds have a single list: an award or completion list for 2019 and 2021 to 2023, the MoU list for 2024.
- The ministry's counts are slot counts. 25 + 50 + 25 = 100 to 2019; 48 in 2021 with 7 repeats gives 141; 2022 adds 50
  (191) and 2023 adds 50 (241). The script checks each step. Under the award lists 99 distinct places joined to 2019,
  under the MoU lists 98. The 7 repeats of 2021 are named by matching the earlier award lists: Jepara, Lombok Timur,
  Kutai Kartanegara, Sleman, Kota Semarang, Kota Mataram, Kabupaten Pasuruan. Kudus (2018 MoU only) also returns in 2021
  and Singkawang (2017 MoU only) in 2022. 2022, 2023 and 2024 repeat no earlier award-list place.
- 2023 is coded to the 28 Feb 2023 signing. The June 2022 selection letters and the Tabura Pos report give 2022, which
  is a sensitivity check.
- Spellings corrected (as printed in place_as_printed or the note): Probolinggi, Pelembang, Musin Banyuasing, Mataran,
  "Kota Pelalawan", "Kabupatet Banyuasin", "Kota Kabupaten Mimika", "Lombok timur", "Payakumbuh" (no Kota), "Bone Bolanga".
- Provinces were added by hand as at the announcement date (Mimika in Papua, Raja Ampat in West Papua before the 2022
  split; Boven Digoel in South Papua by 2023). Toba Samosir was renamed Toba in 2020; the 2021 list prints the old name.
  All are checked against the boundary file when units are linked.
