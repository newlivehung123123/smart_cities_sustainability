Coding notes:
- 87 rows, 85 project_ids. Counts checked by script: 2 pilot cities [1]; 10 Smart Challenge places in 2019 ([3] and [4], as listed in [5]);
  7 main projects in 2020 [5]; 18 projects in the 2020 round [6]; 28 places selected 2019 to 2020; 23 diffusion places [7]; 6 main
  projects in 2021 [8]; 8 campus and 4 town projects [9]; 45 projects in 2021 [10].
- Programme model implementation support throughout: every round funds projects in the selected places with national money.
- Announcement date is the embargo or online release date (day precision). Every round falls in January to June, so
  first_treated_year is the release year.
- The 2019 town master plans [3] were a separate grant (테마형 특화단지 종합계획 수립 지원) but [5] counts them among the 2019 Smart
  Challenge preliminary projects (renamed Town Challenge), so they are coded with the Smart Challenge.
- Main projects in 2020 [5] and 2021 [8] repeat places already selected as preliminary projects; only the first selection sets the
  treated year. They are kept to show which places went on to the larger grant.
- Joint proposals (Gongju and Buyeo) have one row per municipality and one project_id.
- area_scope: part where the release names an area inside the place (both pilot cities, which were new towns under construction;
  the 2019 town and city areas; the four town areas of [5]; Changwon and Seosan towns, Seosan islands and Geoje in [6]; Busan in [8];
  every campus project). Jeju and Chungcheongbuk-do are prefecture. All other rows are whole.
- The 2019 construction programme [2] and the 2021 campus challenge [9] are not in design Table 1 and are kept for sensitivity checks only.
- Not coded: the integrated platform programme (통합플랫폼), smart urban regeneration (스마트도시형 도시재생), regional hub (지역거점) and
  강소형 programmes, and the 2018 to 2020 special plan footnote in [1] except where [5] shows it became the Smart Challenge.
- Design Table 1 correction: "45 sites" is the 2021 round only; 2019 to 2021 together give 73 Smart Challenge selections
  (10 + 18 + 45), some of them repeat selections of the same place.
