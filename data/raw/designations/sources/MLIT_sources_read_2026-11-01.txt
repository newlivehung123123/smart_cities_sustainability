Coding notes:
- Leading projects (先行モデル) receive the demonstration budget; priority projects (重点事業化促進) receive experts and planning support. Both are coded as implementation support, with the batch kept so either can be dropped.
- The 71 partners (別紙3) are not coded as treated: partnership needed only "一定のレベルと意欲" and gave no project support of its own.
- 対象区域 other than 全域 is coded area_scope = part. 島原半島 (Shimabara project) is coded wider; only 島原市 is named as a municipal partner.
- Two projects span two municipalities (熱海市・下田市; 精華町・木津川市); each municipality has its own row with a shared project_id.

Joint selection from FY2021 (japan_joint_selection.csv)
From FY2021, MLIT selects its smart city projects through one Cabinet Office joint review (スマートシティ関連事業合同審査会)
covering five programmes. Each round's press release and annex 2 (別紙2, table of regions with one symbol per programme)
are saved as CAO_smartcity_joint_<date>_press.html and _bessi2.pdf; annex 3 (detail table) where it exists.
The annexes are the PDFs linked from each press page, under https://www8.cao.go.jp/cstp/stmain/pdf/.
