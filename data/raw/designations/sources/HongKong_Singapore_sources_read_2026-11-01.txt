Coding notes:
- programme_model is "whole-city strategy" for the city-states. They are estimated with augmented synthetic control and
  synthetic difference-in-differences (design section 6.3), not in the pooled comparison of programme models.
- Singapore: Smart Nation launch, 24 Nov 2014, first treated 2015. The vision was first set out at the National Day Rally
  of 17 Aug 2014, which also gives 2015, so the coding does not depend on which date is used. Singapore's ASEAN Smart
  Cities Network membership (2018, ascn.csv) comes later and does not change the first treated year.
- Hong Kong, main specification: Blueprint released 15 Dec 2017, first treated 2018. Blueprint 2.0 (10 Dec 2020) continues
  the Blueprint and is not coded as a new designation.
- Hong Kong, sensitivity check: the Kowloon East pilot area (2015 Policy Address, delivered 14 Jan 2015) moves the first
  treated year to 2015. It is a pilot area inside one district, announced to explore feasibility; the feasibility study's
  public engagement began on 7 Nov 2016. Under the main coding, 2015 to 2017 count as pre-treatment years, so this check
  shows whether that partial exposure moves the Hong Kong estimate.
