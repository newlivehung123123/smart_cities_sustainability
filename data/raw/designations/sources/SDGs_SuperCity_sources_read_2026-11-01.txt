Coding notes:
- Announcement date is the press embargo date where one is printed (2020 to 2024), otherwise the release date.
  Every round falls in May to July, so first_treated_year is the round year except 2019 (1 July) and 2020 (17 July),
  which move to 2020 and 2021.
- SDGs Future Cities = certification: a label awarded to proposing municipalities. Each row notes that the 2024 summary
  (別紙1) allows one further application to the regional revitalisation grant (地方創生推進交付金) outside the usual cap.
- Municipal SDGs Model Projects = implementation support: a subsidy to the selected project, up to 20 million yen
  per city in 2024 (別紙1).
- Counts checked by script: 206 Future City project_ids 2018 to 2024 and 70 Model Project project_ids, as in [9];
  every round matches its press release; every Model Project city is also a Future City.
- 大阪府・大阪市 (2020, both programmes) becomes two rows, Osaka Prefecture (area_scope prefecture) and Osaka city,
  with one project_id.
- A row naming only a prefecture is area_scope prefecture; [9] colours the whole prefecture in that case.
- Sado (Model Project 2023, Future City 2022) and Amami (Model Project 2024, Future City 2023) carry notes, per [7] and [9].
- 2025 rows are noted "after the panel".
- The radical ⻄ in the PDF text is replaced by 西.
- place_en is Hepburn without macrons from the MIC kana: ou, oo become o and uu becomes u; ン before b, m, p becomes m.
  Overrides: 広陵町 Koryo (MIC prints ｺｳﾘﾖｳ with a large ﾖ) and 群馬県 Gunma.

Coding notes:
- Announcement date 10 March 2022 (council decision), so first_treated_year = 2022; the cabinet order of 12 April 2022
  is recorded in the note and gives the same year.
- Tsukuba is area_scope whole; Osaka is part (Yumeshima and Umekita phase 2), flagged.
- Coded certification for now: the release names digitalisation and regulatory reform and no funding. [12] records
  Cabinet Office survey projects (調査事業) adopted on 20 July 2022 and 7 July 2023, but the places funded are not
  named there, and the Super City index page (kokusentoc/supercity/index.html) returned "Page Not Found" on 1 Nov 2026.
  If those projects went to the designated zones, the rule in dataset_plan.md section 2 makes Super City
  implementation support. The main specification codes certification and a check codes implementation
  support (dataset_plan.md section 6).
- The three Digital Garden Health Special Zones are not in the design's Table 1 and are kept for a sensitivity check
  only; they share project_id DH2022-1.
