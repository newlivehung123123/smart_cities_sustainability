Sources for macau_smart_city.csv and for the economies with no national designation list (Mongolia, Timor-Leste,
Brunei, Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar), read 1 Nov 2026. Built and checked by scripts/designations/11_macau_others.py.
Passages are copied from the saved text (macau_saved/*.txt and others_saved/*.txt, made from the html or PDF saved the
same day). The ASEAN Smart Cities Network members of these economies are in ascn.csv, not here: Bandar Seri Begawan;
Battambang, Phnom Penh and Siem Reap (2018) and Sihanoukville (2024); Luang Prabang and Vientiane; Mandalay, Nay Pyi Taw
and Yangon (all 2018 unless stated); Dili (2026, after the panel ends).

Searches run on 1 Nov 2026 (web search, English) for a national list of smart city places in each economy: Mongolia
(national programme, government resolution), Cambodia (Ministry of Interior, pilot list beyond ASCN), Laos (Ministry of
Public Works and Transport, decree), Myanmar (Ministry of Construction, selected cities), Brunei (Digital Economy
Masterplan 2025, designation of the capital), Timor-Leste (national programme). None returned a government list naming
places. Search results pointed to a national roadmap and a Ministry of Interior steering committee in Cambodia and to
sub-national action plans for Siem Reap and Preah Sihanouk, and to Brunei's nationwide Digital Economy Masterplan 2025;
these pages were not saved, and the places concerned are ASCN members already coded or the whole of Brunei.

Coding notes:
- Macau is coded "whole-city strategy" from the 4 August 2017 signing (first treated 2018). Macau is one unit and
  is not estimated; the row only documents why Macau is kept out of the Singapore and Hong Kong donor pools.
- Mongolia, Timor-Leste, Brunei, Cambodia, Laos and Myanmar have no national designation list. Their treated places are
  their ASCN members; their other urban districts are untreated. Mongolia has no treated unit, so under economy-by-year
  fixed effects its districts add no comparison within Mongolia; they stay in the Singapore and Hong Kong donor pools.
  Timor-Leste's only treated place (Dili, 2026) is treated after the panel ends.
- Plans by a city or province itself (the Ulaanbaatar master plan, the Luang Namtha and Oudomxay plan, Siem Reap and
  Preah Sihanouk action plans) are self-adoption, not national designation, and are not coded, as for Vietnam's
  provincial schemes.
