Coding notes:
- One row per project (37), matching [1]. Counts per notice 5, 10, 15, 6, 1; the first three notices give 30 projects in
  23 provinces and the first four 36 in 25, as in [8] (checked by script).
- Announcement date is the date the notice was signed: each notice awards the logo "from now on" (2021 to 2023), and the
  depa page dates the batches by these signing dates. The approving meeting date is in the note. Every notice falls in
  July to December, so first_treated_year is the next year (2022, 2022, 2023, 2024; 2026 for Phuket Tinicon Valley, after
  the panel). Using the meeting date instead would move only notice 1/2564 (meeting 6 May 2021) to 2021.
- Programme model certification: a logo valid two years; no notice names funding. [6] is filed as "smartcitygrant" but is
  an award of the logo.
- place_as_printed is the local government named in the notice (เทศบาล, อบต.) if any, otherwise the province.
  area_scope: part where the project line names an area smaller than that place (Mae Moh, Wangchan Valley, Samyan,
  Rama 4, the area around Khlong Phadung Krung Kasem, Makkasan, Chiang Mai old town, Chiang Mai University, Tinicon Valley);
  prefecture where only a province is named (13 rows, project area to be read from the executive summaries when units are
  linked); whole where a local government is named.
- The 16 projects renewed by [7] carry a note. The other 20 logos from 2021 to 2023 were not renewed in that notice;
  treatment stays absorbing under dataset_plan.md section 2.
- The 190 promotion zones (เขตส่งเสริม) in [1] are not coded: they are declared zones, not awards of the logo.
