2023: Chiang Mai, Khon Kaen, and Rayong (Thailand)
2024: Sumedang (Indonesia) and Sihanoukville City (Cambodia)
2025: Ipoh, Putrajaya, and Seberang Perai (Malaysia); Cauayan City (the Philippines); Denpasar and Semarang (Indonesia)
2026: Dili (Timor-Leste)."
