Coding notes:
- Digital Cities 2025 promotes locations for IT and business process management (IT-BPM) investment. It is not a smart
  city programme: its criteria are talent, infrastructure, cost and business environment, and its support is for the
  IT-BPM industry. The main specification therefore does not code it: the 25 locations are untreated in the Philippines
  unless they are ASEAN Smart Cities Network members (none of the 25 is).
- Two sensitivity checks use these rows: (a) the 25 locations are coded treated from 2020, as implementation support
  (DICT and IBPAP support in four areas; no transfer to the cities is stated); (b) the 25 locations are dropped from the
  control group, because DICT connectivity and digital government support may affect them.
- Date is the launch, 30 June 2020 (both news reports). Under the half-year rule (month 6 or earlier) the first treated
  year is 2020, so the launch sits on the rule's boundary.
- The three clusters give one row per local government unit with a shared project_id (25 project_ids, 31 rows).
  place_as_printed is the name in the GMA list; place_en is the name in the primer's profile heading. Units in the
  Philippines are linked when the unit file is built (geoBoundaries ADM2 in the Philippines are provinces, so the unit
  level for the Philippines is decided then).
- The seven roadmap cities are flagged in the note; the flag is descriptive and not used to define treatment.
- Centers of Excellence and Next Wave Cities are earlier IT-BPM location labels (Next Wave Cities since 2009, per the
  primer), not part of the 2020 selection, and are not coded. New Clark City is a construction project of the Bases
  Conversion and Development Authority, not a designation of existing places, and is not coded.
